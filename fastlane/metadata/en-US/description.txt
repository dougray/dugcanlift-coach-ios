LIFT Coach is the trainer's side of LIFT. Your clients log their training and food in the LIFT app; this is where you read those logs and send them a plan.

You need a client who uses LIFT. Coach does not log workouts or food itself, and it cannot read data from other apps. When a client taps "Send to Coach" in LIFT, they send you a link. Paste that link into Coach and their log appears on your roster.

YOUR ROSTER
Every client you have pasted a link from, with when they last logged. Paste a newer link at any time to bring a client up to date.

EACH CLIENT
- Training volume, day by day
- Fuel: calories against the goal the client set
- Bodyweight trend
- Estimated one-rep max for each lift, kept separate by equipment
- Runs, walks and hikes, personal bests, and their last route on a map when the client chose to share it
- Weights shown in pounds or kilograms, whichever the client uses

TRAIN
Build workout templates with sets, reps, weight, RPE, time or distance. Book them onto a client's week, then send the week to the client as a link that opens in LIFT.

COOK
Keep a recipe library with ingredients and macros. Add recipes by hand, from a built-in catalogue, by searching TheMealDB, from a recipe web page's address, or by pasting a caption and editing the split. Plan a client's breakfasts, lunches, dinners and snacks for the week and send the plan with the workouts. Each client gets a shopping list built from their planned meals, with their own ticks.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
There is no account and no server. Everything stays on your iPhone. A client's log reaches you only as a link they chose to send. The only times Coach goes online are when you search TheMealDB or import a recipe page you pasted, and neither request includes anything about you or your clients.

Save a backup file to move your roster, recipes and workouts to another device, or import your library from the LIFT Coach web app.

Light and dark appearance, following your phone's setting or your choice.
